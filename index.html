Features

Every MM2 tool you need, in one hub

Six focused scripts built for Murder Mystery 2 — clean, lightweight, and compatible with popular Windows executors.

🎯

Aimbot

Smoothed target locking for the murderer and sheriff with adjustable FOV, prediction, and hotkey toggles for clean, controllable aim.

👁️

ESP

See every role through walls — murderer, sheriff, and innocents — with name tags, distance readouts, and color-coded box highlights.

🧲

Auto-Pickup

Automatically grabs dropped knives, coins, and gun drops the moment they spawn, so you're always armed before the next encounter.

🪙

Coin Farm

Idle-friendly coin farming loop that collects round rewards and repeats matches efficiently, with a configurable stop condition.

🔪

Knife Throw Assist

Trajectory prediction for thrown knives with lead compensation — land long-range throws on moving targets far more consistently.

🧩

Executor Ready

Single-file Lua scripts with a built-in config menu. Paste, execute, and toggle modules — no dependencies or extra loaders required.
